Inside the model

What we design — so you don't have to.

Every component below is built from your vehicle's specific specs. Nothing is templated. The block diagrams are from real work — this is what the inside of your flight control system looks like.

01

6-DOF Rigid Body Dynamics

Full equations of motion — forces, moments, mass, and inertia — built from your airframe geometry and operating conditions.

02

Propulsion & Motor Model

Thrust curves, torque response, and RPM dynamics for your specific motors and propeller configuration.

03

Sensor & IMU Model

Gyroscope and accelerometer noise, bias drift, and positional error — so the simulation behaves like real hardware in the field.

04

Flight Control System

Attitude and position controllers designed to your performance requirements — settling time, overshoot, and steady-state error across your operating envelope.

05

Simulation Environment

Wind disturbance, payload variation, and motor failure scenarios — the conditions that reveal exactly where your controller breaks spec before hardware does.
